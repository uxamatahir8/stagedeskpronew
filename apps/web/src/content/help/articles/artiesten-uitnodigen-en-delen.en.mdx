## Adding an artist

Under *Artists* choose **Add artist**: stage name, genres, photo and the default share per booking.

## Inviting them to the app

Invite the artist under *Team* with the role *Artist*. They then see their gigs, accept or decline requests and see their earnings.

## Sharing with a partner agency

Working with another agency? Share an artist from their profile. Once accepted, the partner agency can ask the artist for its own bookings. The artist sees those gigs among their own.
