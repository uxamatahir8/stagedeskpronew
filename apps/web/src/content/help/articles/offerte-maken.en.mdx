Open a booking and choose **Create quote**.

1. Add lines from your services, or type them. For each line choose quantity, price and VAT rate.
2. Choose the deposit percentage and how long the quote is valid.
3. Choose **Send quote**. The customer gets an email with a link to view and accept it.

If you change a sent quote, you make a new version. The customer always sees the newest.

Once the customer accepts, the booking is *Booked* and you can ask an artist.
