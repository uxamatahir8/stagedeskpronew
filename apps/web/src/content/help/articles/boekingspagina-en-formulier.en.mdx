Every agency gets its own booking page, for example `stagedesk.pro/b/your-agency`. Customers choose the kind of party, the date and their wishes there. The inquiry shows up straight away under *Needs attention* on your home page, and you get an email.

## The form on your own website

Want the form on your own site? Copy the snippet under *Booking page* and paste it where the form should go:

```
<script src="https://stagedesk.pro/embed.js" data-agency="your-agency" data-lang="en" async></script>
```

The form adapts to the width of your page.

## Spam

The form checks that a person is filling it in, without puzzles for your customer.
