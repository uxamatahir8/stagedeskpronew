After signing up you create your agency. Then it pays to set up these four things.

1. **Agency details**: under *Agency settings*: name, address, KvK and VAT numbers, logo and brand colour. They appear on your quotes and invoices.
2. **Services**: under *Services*: your standard lines, such as "DJ, per hour" or "Travel". You add them to a quote with one click.
3. **Your team**: under *Team*: invite admins, staff and artists. Everyone only sees what belongs to their role.
4. **Your booking page**: under *Booking page*: switch it on, choose your texts and share the link.

During your trial you can use everything. Under *Billing* you see how long it runs and choose a plan.
