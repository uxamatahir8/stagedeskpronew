A booked date is only really booked once money has come in. A deposit protects your schedule: you don't turn down other inquiries for a customer who later backs out.

## How much to ask

Most agencies ask 25 to 30 percent. Enough to be serious, not so much that a customer hesitates. In StageDesk you set the default percentage and can change it per quote.

## When does the customer pay the rest?

Agree on a fixed moment, for example two weeks before the event. StageDesk sends the customer a reminder with the amount still open on that day.

## How the money reaches you and your artist

With online payments the customer pays by iDEAL or card. After the event, and a short wait for any disputes, StageDesk splits the amount:

- the artist's share goes to the artist,
- the platform fee goes to StageDesk,
- the rest goes to your agency.

The split is fixed when the customer accepts the quote. If rates change later, that only applies to new bookings.

## What if a customer cancels?

You can refund a payment fully or partly. If the money was already paid out, your share and the artist's are taken back proportionally.

Prefer offline? That works too: record the payment in the booking and StageDesk keeps track of what's still open.
