## Signing in with a code

On the sign-in page you can choose **Sign in with a code**. You get a six-digit code by email, valid for ten minutes. Customers always sign in this way.

## Forgot your password

Choose **Forgot password** and follow the link in the email.

## Two-factor authentication

Under your profile you turn on two-factor authentication with an app such as Google Authenticator or 1Password. Keep the recovery codes somewhere safe. Lost them? Contact support.
