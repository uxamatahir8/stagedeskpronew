Not every customer pays online. Manual payments let you track bank transfers and cash, with a clear confirmation of receipt.

## Setting up payment methods

Go to **Settings → Payments** and choose which methods you offer customers: online (iDEAL and card), bank transfer or cash. For bank transfer you enter your IBAN and account name. They appear on the quote and in the payment email.

## The customer reports a payment

After a transfer the customer chooses **I've paid** in the customer portal, with date and reference. You get a notification and the payment appears in **Payments** under *To confirm*.

## Confirming receipt

Check your bank account and choose **Confirm receipt**. The payment is added to the booking and the customer automatically gets a receipt.

If the money hasn't arrived, choose **Not received** and write a short note. The customer gets it by email and can report the payment again.

## Recording a payment yourself

Paid in cash, or the customer didn't report anything? Open the booking and choose **Record payment**. Enter the amount, method and date.

## Good to know

- Payments that wait a long time for confirmation get the label *Waiting long*.
- The open amount of a booking only counts confirmed payments.
