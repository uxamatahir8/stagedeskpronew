The calendar shows all your events per month. Artists mark themselves when they can't play.

## Your agency's calendar

Go to **Calendar**. Every booking with a date is in it, coloured by status. Click an event to open the booking. On busy days you see *+3 more*; click it for the full list.

## Artist availability

Artists open **Availability** in their app and tap the days they can't play. If you ask an artist for a date on which they're unavailable, you see a warning.

## Avoiding double bookings

If an artist already has a gig on the same day, StageDesk tells you when you ask them. You can still go ahead on purpose, for example for an afternoon and an evening show.

## The calendar in your own calendar app

Artists can subscribe to their gigs in Google Calendar, Apple Calendar or Outlook. Changes come through automatically.
