Every booking has its own conversation, so everything you agree with the customer and the artist is in one place.

## Who sees what

A booking has two conversations: one with the customer and one with the artist. The customer never sees the conversation with the artist, and vice versa.

## Sending messages

Open the booking and choose the **Messages** tab. Type your message and send. Customers and artists get an email with the message and a link to reply. If they reply in the app or the portal, you see it right away.

## Notifications

New messages show at the bell at the top and in the bookings list. In your profile you choose which notifications you also want by email.

## Attachments and agreements

Important agreements, like a run sheet or start time, are best kept in the notes or fields of the booking itself. They stay visible even when the conversation gets long.
