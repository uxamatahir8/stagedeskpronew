Your customers get their own portal for their event. They don't need to create an account: they get in through the link in the email.

## What the customer sees

- The quote, with the button to accept it.
- The status of the event and who will perform once that's confirmed.
- What has been paid and what's still open, with the button to pay or report a bank transfer.
- The conversation with your agency.

## Paying

For online payment the customer chooses iDEAL or card. For a bank transfer the customer sees your IBAN and the reference, and reports the payment with **I've paid**.

## After the event

Afterwards StageDesk asks the customer for a review. You can show reviews on your booking page and your website.

## Lost the link?

The customer can request a new code with their email address under **Sign in** and gets back into the portal.
