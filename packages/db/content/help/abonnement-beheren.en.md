You manage your subscription yourself under **Subscription**.

## The trial

Every new agency starts with a free trial with all features. You don't need to enter payment details. At the end you choose a plan; if you don't, you can still view everything but not change it.

## Choosing or switching a plan

Choose **Change plan** and select a plan. An upgrade starts immediately and you pay pro rata for the rest of the period. A downgrade starts at the next renewal.

## Usage and limits

On the same page you see how many artists, team seats and bookings you use. When you're close to a limit you get a notice before anything stops working.

## Invoices

All invoices for your subscription are at the bottom of the page as PDF.

## Cancelling

Choose **Cancel subscription**. You keep access until the end of the paid period. Your data is kept, so you can start again later without losing anything.
