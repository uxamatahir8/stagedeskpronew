Artists work in their own app, on their phone or computer. They only see their own gigs.

## Invitation and signing in

You invite an artist from **Artists**. The artist gets an email, creates an account and can then sign in with a code by email, no password needed.

## Gigs

**Gigs** lists requests and confirmed gigs. Accepting or declining a request takes one tap. For each gig the artist sees the date, times, venue, contact person and the agreed fee.

## Availability

In **Availability** the artist taps the days they can't play. The agency sees this when planning.

## Earnings and payouts

**Earnings** shows what the artist earned per gig and what has been paid out. With online payments the artist connects their own Stripe account for automatic payouts.

## Working for several agencies

An artist can work for several agencies with one account. Each agency only sees its own gigs.
