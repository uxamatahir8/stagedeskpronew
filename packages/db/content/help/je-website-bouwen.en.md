With the website builder you create your own agency website, without a designer or developer. Inquiries arrive straight in StageDesk.

## Getting started

Go to **Website**. You start with a complete example site with your agency name, your artists and your services. Then change whatever you like.

## Adding and dragging sections

A page is made of sections: an opening, your artists, services, reviews, frequently asked questions, the inquiry form and more. Choose **Add section** and drag sections into the order you want. Each section has its own text, images and style.

## Colours and font

Under **Theme** you choose your colours, font and logo. The whole site adapts.

## Pages

Besides the home page you can create extra pages, like About us or a page per type of party. They're added to the menu automatically.

## Publishing

You work in a draft. Only when you choose **Publish** do visitors see the new version. You can restore earlier versions.

## Your address

Your site is live straight away on a free StageDesk address. To connect your own domain, read [Connecting your own domain](/help/eigen-domein-koppelen).
