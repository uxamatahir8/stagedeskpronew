Working with more people on your agency? Invite them and give each the role that fits.

## Inviting someone

Go to **Team** and choose **Invite member**. Enter the email address and pick a role. They get an email with a link to join. An invitation is valid for seven days and you can withdraw it while it's open.

## The roles

| Role | What they can do |
| --- | --- |
| Owner | Everything, including the subscription, payments and the team |
| Manager | Everything except the subscription and changing the owner |
| Planner | Bookings, quotes, artists and calendar |

Artists and customers aren't team members. They get their own app and portal.

## Changing a role or removing someone

In the list, choose a different role for a member, or **Remove**. A removed member loses access immediately. What they did stays visible in the history.

## Team seats

How many team members you can have depends on your plan. You see the usage under **Subscription**.
