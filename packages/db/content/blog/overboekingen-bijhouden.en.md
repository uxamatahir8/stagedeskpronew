Online payment is easiest, but many customers still pay for a party by bank transfer. Here's how to avoid chasing payments.

## One reference per booking

Ask customers to use the booking number as the reference. Then you immediately see on your statement which party a payment belongs to. StageDesk puts the reference on the quote and in the payment email.

## Let the customer report the payment

In the customer portal the customer reports a transfer with date and reference. You get a notification and only need to check that the money arrived.

## Confirm receipt, and say so when it's wrong

A short confirmation with a receipt reassures the customer. Don't see anything coming in? Say so right away, with a note. The sooner, the less awkward.

## Remind in time

Agree on a fixed date for the remaining amount, for example two weeks before the party. StageDesk sends a reminder with the open amount automatically.

## Keep one overview

Keep all payments, online and manual, on the booking. Then you see at any time what's open, even when a colleague created the booking.
