A booking agency without a good website misses inquiries. Customers search for "wedding DJ Utrecht" and decide within a minute whether to keep looking. What should your site have at least?

## Show who performs

Customers book people, not an agency. Put your artists up front with a photo, a short text and a clip. One good photo per artist works better than a gallery of twenty.

## Give a starting price

"Price on request" feels like a hurdle. A starting price per package filters out customers who don't fit and gives the rest confidence.

## Make inquiries easy

A form with date, type of party, venue and number of guests is enough. Don't ask for more than you need for a first quote. With StageDesk every inquiry arrives as a booking, with the date already in your calendar.

## Reviews count

Real reviews from earlier customers are the strongest argument on your site. Ask for one right after the party, while the customer is still enthusiastic.

## Fast and good on mobile

More than half of your visitors are on a phone. A site that loads slowly or where the form doesn't fit costs you bookings.

## Your own domain

Your own domain like youragency.com looks more professional and is easier to find. In StageDesk you connect it in a few minutes.
